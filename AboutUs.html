Who We Are

Mangaung Digital Art Print is a registered community focused Non Profit Organisation located in Section A Botishabelo. We believe in the power of an active communinity and strive to uplift our people through creative media and art.

Our Mission

Our primary focus is to driveyouth empowermentandjob creation within Mangung We achieve this ny providing access to modern creative tools, training and support.

What we Focus On

  • Professional Training:Giving our community a voice through digital art and photography.
  • Artistic Expression:Using graphic design and print technology to prepare our youth for the workplace.
