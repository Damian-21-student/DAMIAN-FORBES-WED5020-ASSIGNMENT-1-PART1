About Mngaung Digital Art Print

Who We Are

Mangaung Digital Art Print is a registered community focused Non Profit Organisation located in Section A Botishabelo.
We believe in the power of an active communinity and strive to uplift our people through creative media and art.
Mangaung Digital Art Print is a community focused non-profit organization that was founded in 2018
the organization which was founded by grassroots artist in the area uses
digital print visual arts and creative training as educational tools to uplift underprivileged youngsters
and provide them with an alternate path away from high school dropout rates poverty and violence

Our Mission

Our primary focus is to drive youth empowerment and job creation within Mangung We achieve this ny providing access to modern creative tools, training and support.
Mangaung Digital Art Print provides youth and students in underprivileged communities with high-quality creative projects and easily accessible digital print services.
By use art as an alternative intervention, we hope to reduce school dropout rate technological digital skills and promote long-lasting socio-economic empowerment at the local level.

Our Vision

To create an inclusive technologically advanced creative hub in the Free State where media and the arts are the primary means of reducing juvenile vulnerability , nurturuing outstanding local talent , and creating a self creative economy.

What we Focus On

  • Digital Skills Development:Equipping the youth with the knowledge to navigate modern software.
  • Professional Training:Giving our community a voice through digital art and photography.
  • Artistic Expression:Using graphic design and print technology to prepare our youth for the workplace.
